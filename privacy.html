Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to Playmaker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data on our static game website.

1. Data Storage (Local Storage)

All gameplay data, including your win/loss statistics, played counts, current streaks, and best streaks, are saved locally in your browser's local storage (namespaced as footy_v2_*). This data stays entirely on your device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Web Analytics & Monitoring

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to compile aggregated statistics, such as visitor count, page views, and gameplay completion metrics. This helps us monitor performance and optimize game rules.

Furthermore, we track runtime errors and promise exceptions to catch bugs. No personally identifiable information (PII) is captured.

3. User Consent & Opt-Out

You have full control over analytics tracking. When you visit Playmaker for the first time, you are asked to accept or decline cookies. If you decline, Google Analytics scripts will not load, and no tracking information will be transmitted.

4. Contact & Legal Links

For our service terms, please review our Terms & Conditions. If you have questions or feedback regarding this policy, feel free to contact us via our GitHub repository issues panel or our social media channels.
