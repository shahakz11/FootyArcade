What is This & That?

This & That is a daily football trivia challenge by Playmaker where fans identify every footballer who satisfies two distinct career criteria simultaneously. Puzzles feature dual-club representations, national heritage pairings, multi-league champions, and iconic tournament winners. Use the autocomplete dropdown to guess qualifying players before exhausting your lives. Puzzles refresh daily at midnight UTC with complete streak statistics and playable back-in-time archives.

Database Coverage: Official senior appearances, transfers, and honours from 1990 up to June 2026 alongside verified historic legends.

HOW TO PLAY

Objective: Name every footballer who matches BOTH statements displayed at the top of the board.

Guessing: Type any player's name and select them from the autocomplete dropdown list.

Hints: Click the lightbulb on any locked card to uncover nationality and position clues.

Lives & Strikes: You have 5 lives. An incorrect guess loses 1 life. Guessing a player who matches only one statement is a strike!

VAR Review: Appeal disputed answers with the automated video assistant referee to verify real-world career records.

Card Reveal: Each correct footballer flips their card on the board, revealing their photo, details, and verified career evidence.

Surrender: If you're stuck, you can give up at any time to reveal all remaining answers.

Database Scope: Official senior appearances, transfers, and honours from 1990 up to June 2026 alongside verified historic legends.
