HOW TO PLAY

1. Anyone But: At the top, you'll see today's excluded star. They fit all 3 tables below, but you CANNOT guess them!

2. 3 Criteria Tables: Each table represents a career milestone, club combination, or goal count. Name other players who satisfy each table.

3. Multi-Hit Crossovers: A player who fits multiple tables will automatically fill slots across all matching tables with a single guess!

4. Lives: You start with 5 lives. An incorrect guess deducts 1 life. (Guessing the excluded star will remind you without costing a life!)

5. Goal: Fill as many slots as you can before lives run out!

What is Anyone But?

Anyone But is a daily inverse football trivia puzzle by Playmaker where fans name footballers who satisfy three distinct career milestone tables while avoiding one iconic excluded star. Each daily challenge highlights an excluded player alongside three simultaneous criteria categories — such as shared club histories, transfer valuation milestones, or career goal tallies. Players submit footballer names to uncover matching slots across all three tables. Guesses that match multiple criteria simultaneously trigger multi-hit combos, unlocking slots across multiple tables with a single submission.

Database Coverage: Official senior appearances and transfers from 1990 up to June 2026.

VAR Verification: Players can submit any disputed answer to the automated VAR engine to appeal real-world transfer and appearance records.
