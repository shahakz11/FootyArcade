FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Playmaker?

Playmaker is a free daily football trivia arcade. Every day, new challenges test your knowledge of transfers, career paths, and top scorers from leagues worldwide. No sign-up required.

How do you play Playmaker?

Choose a daily challenge, type player or club names into the search box, and guess correctly before your lives run out. Track your streak and share Wordle-style results with friends.

Is Playmaker free to play?

Yes, Playmaker is completely free. No sign-up required. Your progress is saved locally in your browser.

How often are new puzzles added?

Every day at midnight UTC, all games receive a fresh puzzle with new data. You can also play the last 7 days of past puzzles via the back-in-time feature.

What data does Playmaker use?

Playmaker uses verified transfer and goalscoring data sourced from Transfermarkt, covering the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, and more. Updated to July 2026.

What is the football version of Wordle?

Playmaker offers Wordle-style daily football challenges with shareable emoji results, streak tracking, and multiple game modes including transfer quizzes, career path games, and goalscorer trivia.
