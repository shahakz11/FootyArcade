HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Unmask the mystery Player of the Day by identifying players who match an expanding chain of career clubs.

1. Sequential Progression: Start with 1 club constraint. Each step unlocks one at a time, adding another club to the chain.

2. 🎯 Instant Win: If you guess the Player of the Day at any stage, the game ends immediately in victory!

3. Hints: Click the Hint buttons on the mystery player card to reveal their Nationality or Position.

4. No Repeat Guesses: Once you submit a player, they cannot be used again in that session.

5. Skipping: Stuck on a tricky club combination? Click Skip to unlock the next step and keep playing.

6. Lives: Guessing an invalid player who does not meet all active constraints costs 1 life.
